Privacy Policy

Application: HR Email Automation

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Overview

HR Email Automation is a personal tool designed to help its operator prepare and send job application emails to recruitment contacts.

2. Information Used

The application may process recruiter email addresses, names, job-related information, email subjects, message content and the operator's resume.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to prepare and manage job application emails and their attachments. Emails are sent only when the operator explicitly enables and confirms sending.

4. Gmail Access

The application uses Google Gmail services to send emails through the operator's authorized Gmail account. It does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising.

5. Data Storage and Sharing

Contact information and application records may be stored locally on the operator's computer. Email content and attachments are shared with the intended recipients through Gmail. Information is not sold to third parties.

6. Security

The operator is responsible for protecting account credentials, OAuth tokens, local files and access to the computer.

7. Data Retention

Local contact records and email logs may be retained by the operator and can be deleted from the local computer.
